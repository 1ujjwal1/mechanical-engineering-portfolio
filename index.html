PROFILE

Vehicle dynamics, from geometry to performance.

Mechanical engineering at NSUT · Team TDR Off-Road Racing · Baja / ATVC vehicle development

I want to understand why a vehicle behaves the way it does—and use that understanding to make better design choices.

My focus is the connection between suspension geometry, tire-road interaction, packaging and vehicle performance. In Team TDR, I have worked on camber, caster, toe and KPI, evaluated setups under simulated load cases, and contributed to drivetrain and tire-pressure decisions for off-road performance. Across CAD, analysis and competition work, I value clear assumptions, practical manufacturability and learning from failures rather than treating one simulation result as final proof.
