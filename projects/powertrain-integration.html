CASE STUDY · SYSTEM INTEGRATION & ANALYSIS

EV Powertrain
Integration.

Integrated the gearbox, sprocket-and-chain transmission, shafts and supporting components within the Baja vehicle assembly. The work combines packaging with separate gear and sprocket analysis, while keeping the current reduction ratios adjustable as vehicle requirements evolve.

01 / Vehicle installation

Powertrain in the vehicle assembly

The digital assembly was used to check the drivetrain path and available space inside the chassis, including the motor/gearbox location, chain run and surrounding frame members.

Power path overview: motor → gearbox → drive sprocket → chain → driven sprocket / axle. Clearances, alignment, mounting support and maintenance access must be checked together, not as isolated parts.
02 / Gearbox architecture

Gearbox assembly and gear analysis

The gearbox was assembled from its internal components to review the gear stages, shafts, bearings and casing interfaces. An exploded assembly view helps show how the parts relate and where assembly or packaging conflicts may occur.

The current gearbox reduction used for integration is 10:1. The ratio remains a design input that can be revisited if the required wheel torque, motor operating range or event target changes.

03 / Final drive

Sprocket, chain and axle layout

The chain-drive layout was checked as a complete arrangement rather than only as individual sprockets. The design work includes sprocket positioning, centre distance, chain path and the interface to the gearbox output and driven axle.

The current sprocket reduction is 1.28:1. It is a working configuration and can be changed as performance targets and operating requirements develop.

04 / Analysis and verification

Analysis scope and interpretation

  • Review the gearbox gear-stage arrangement and inspect the supplied gear-pair stress result.
  • Evaluate the sprocket and chain-drive setup using the supplied ANSYS deformation plots.
  • Use the assembly to check overall alignment, space constraints, frame clearance and maintainability.
  • Relate the transmission ratios to the target wheel-speed and wheel-torque requirements once the motor torque-speed data and loaded tyre radius are fixed.
  • Check chain tension, shaft/bearing loads, sprocket support stiffness, fastening and guarding before final release.
Results note: the supplied deformation plots display maximum values of approximately 0.0099709 mm for the sprocket study and 0.0049221 mm for the chain/sprocket setup. These are the values shown in the screenshots; they should not be compared directly or treated as validated design limits until their loads, supports, material properties, contacts and mesh sensitivity are confirmed.
05 / Current status

Current configuration and next checks

The powertrain is represented in the vehicle assembly, with gearbox and final-drive analysis images documented. The current reduction is 10:1 through the gearbox and 1.28:1 at the sprockets, giving a provisional combined reduction of 12.8:1.

The ratios remain adjustable. Final selection should use the motor torque-speed/efficiency data, controller limits, target acceleration, tyre loaded radius, chain tension, shaft and bearing capacity, sprocket alignment and the available adjustment range. The screenshots document analysis work, but do not by themselves establish fatigue life or full operational validation.
